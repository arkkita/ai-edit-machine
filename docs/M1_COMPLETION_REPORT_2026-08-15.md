4. Hardened exact-episode semantics: a discussion about a show cannot place a scene, quote, or intro inside an episode unless the supporting fact itself carries the matching locator.
5. Replaced model-authored emotional/intro rationale with deterministic copy derived from validated focus, source purpose, and moment evidence.
6. Enforced evidence expiry, purge, and mandatory-deletion deadlines on long-running result reads and link opens, not only at startup.
